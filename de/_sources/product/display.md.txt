# The rings

## Front view

```{figure} ../_static/img/rings/rings-front.png
:alt: Score rings around the quawa monster, invented example values

Example with invented values: quawa measured with the danwos model.
```

- **Centre:** the tool's monster or logo.
- **Rings:** level 1 right around the logo, every further level one ring
  further out, up to level 5. Level 0 has no ring.
- **Segments:** one per area, at the same angle on every ring.
- **Reached block:** the reached levels of an area merge into one piece of
  pie from the logo outwards, thick frame, full "good" colour
  ({need}`DEC_REACHED_BLOCK`). Its height is the area's maturity – the
  picture gets hills and valleys.
- **On top:** every level above the block shows its own state: score
  colour, dark grey, dashed or hatched ({need}`DEC_GREY_STATES`).
- **Lines:** bold circle = current target; dash-dot = final target; dotted
  ring = level not targeted. Rings between the two lines are scheduled –
  measured and shown already, in force from their date or release.

One picture answers three questions: where the project stands, where it
wants to go, and what has already been thought about.

## Colours as a seal

```{figure} ../_static/img/rings/rings-compare.png
:alt: The same results in danwos colours and in standard red–green
```

- **danwos scale** – the configuration follows the danwos model: deep
  terracotta `#A8322A` → terracotta `#E2725B` → amber `#E2A93B` → moss
  `#8A9A5B` → logo teal `#14958B`.
- **standard scale** – the configuration deviates: red `#D73027` → yellow
  `#FEE08B` → green `#1A9850`.
- The whole picture switches, not single rings ({need}`DEC_COLOUR_SEAL`).
  When a configuration keeps the danwos colours: see
  [Configuration](configuration.md#when-a-configuration-keeps-the-danwos-colours).
- Neither scale is checked for colour blindness yet; the score in the
  tooltip, the thick frame and the patterns carry the meaning too.

## Slim variants

```{figure} ../_static/img/rings/rings-variants.png
:alt: Full view, only reached levels, and up to the current target
```

| Variant | Shows | Hides | For |
|---|---|---|---|
| **full** | everything | – | the complete picture |
| **reached** | only the reached blocks, thin guide circles, level labels | everything not reached, target lines | "where do we stand" at a glance |
| **target** | everything up to the current target | everything above it – also reached levels and broken measurements ({need}`DEC_TARGET_VARIANT`) | "what do we owe today" |
