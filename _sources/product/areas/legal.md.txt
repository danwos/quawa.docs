# Legal

Licences and data handling are known and fit the rules.

Each level adds measures; a level is reached when its measures and all
measures below meet their thresholds. Measures and levels are fixed by the
danwos model ({need}`DEC_DANWOS_MODEL`); a project only says where the
results come from.

## Level 1 – Sketched

Own licence declared (LICENSE, SPDX headers).

## Level 2 – Shaped

Dependency licences inventoried.

## Level 3 – Solid

Licence check against rules (no copyleft, no proprietary).

## Level 4 – Refined

SBOM and third-party notices per release; data inventory with GDPR notes.

## Level 5 – Audited

Legal review with evidence.

## Possible checks

Examples – which check feeds which measure is the project's choice.

- own licence: LICENSE file, SPDX headers (e.g. reuse lint)
- dependency licences: license-checker, pip-licenses
- SBOM: syft, CycloneDX
- data inventory and GDPR notes as documents
- legal review evidence
