# Requirements

What the tool is meant to do is written down, covered and traced to tests.

Each level adds measures; a level is reached when its measures and all
measures below meet their thresholds. Measures and levels are fixed by the
danwos model ({need}`DEC_DANWOS_MODEL`); a project only says where the
results come from.

## Level 1 – Sketched

Feature descriptions exist for the main features.

## Level 2 – Shaped

Every user-visible feature described (≥ 80 %).

## Level 3 – Solid

Traceability feature → passing test (≥ 80 %).

## Level 4 – Refined

Acceptance criteria per feature, all verified; decisions logged.

## Level 5 – Audited

Requirements reviewed and approved (100 %), change impact visible.

## Possible checks

Examples – which check feeds which measure is the project's choice.

- deciwa / Sphinx-Needs: feature descriptions, their coverage, links feature → test, approvals
